B612 font

The complete license is in assets/fonts/B612-OFL.txt.

Saved-link codec
----------------

The local saved-link codec uses json-url 4.0.0 (ISC), including its LZMA,
MessagePack, and URL-safe Base64 paths. The application deploys only the
browser entry point and chunks required by the LZMA codec. Webpack-generated
license notices are retained beside those JavaScript files in
assets/vendor/json-url/*.LICENSE.txt.

Relevant upstream projects:

- json-url: https://github.com/masotime/json-url
